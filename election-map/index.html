Harris Wins Connecticut

Vice President Kamala Harris won Connecticut in the 2024 presidential election, adding the state’s seven electoral votes to the Democratic column.

Harris received 992,053 votes to Donald Trump’s 736,918, a margin of 255,135 votes. Of the two-party vote, Harris took 57.4 percent and Trump 42.6 percent.

Harris won 102 of Connecticut’s 169 towns, while Trump won 67. Her strongest results came from the state’s largest cities. Stamford gave her the biggest vote total of any town (35,570), followed by New Haven (30,662), Bridgeport (26,992), Norwalk (26,526) and West Hartford (26,254). In New Haven, she beat Trump by more than 24,000 votes, and in Hartford by more than 18,000.

Some towns were extremely close. Andover went to Trump by just three votes (1,035 to 1,032), and Ansonia went to Harris by only 22 votes (3,860 to 3,838). Hampton, Warren and Middlefield were also decided by fewer than 50 votes.
